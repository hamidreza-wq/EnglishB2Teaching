# Content licensing policy

Use original project-authored educational material, or externally sourced material
whose Public Domain/CC0 status has been verified for the intended distribution
jurisdictions. Free access is not permission to redistribute.

Before any import, record the source, author, exact licence/dedication, evidence,
verification date, applicable jurisdiction, modifications and reviewer approval.
Unclear or unverified material must remain excluded. Additional content licences
require explicit owner authorisation after their terms have been reviewed.

Review model weights independently of software licences. Do not copy official
examination papers, textbook extracts, scraped dictionaries or recordings.
Author practice content from first principles and review answer keys separately.
AI assistance does not automatically make content public domain.

No ingestion/approval automation exists in the shell. These rules describe future
requirements, not implemented enforcement or completed legal review.
